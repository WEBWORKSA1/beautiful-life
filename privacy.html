This Privacy Policy explains how Beautiful.Life ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: when you submit a form (newsletter, matching request, contest entry, donation pledge, application or contact message) we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form-processing service.
  • Automatically collected information: like most websites, our hosting provider and advertising partners may collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited.
  • Tool data: our interactive tools store your entries only in your browser's local storage. We cannot see this data. Clear your browser storage to delete it.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and deliver what you requested.
  • To introduce matching requests to up to three relevant professionals, only with your consent.
  • To send newsletters you opted into (unsubscribe at any time).
  • To run contests, process pledges and evaluate applications.
  • To improve the site and maintain security.

Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use essential storage (e.g. your theme and cookie choice), and our advertising partners use cookies as described above. Our cookie banner lets you accept all cookies or limit to essential cookies and non-personalised ads. You can also control cookies in your browser settings.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube may collect data according to its own privacy policy. Links to external news sources are governed by those sites' policies. Our live good-news feed is retrieved through a public RSS conversion service.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help operate the site (hosting, form processing, advertising), with professionals you ask to be matched with, or where required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information and to withdraw consent. To make a request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

Data retention & security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected and use reasonable safeguards to protect them. No online transmission is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.
