About us

Live well. Love well. Do well.

Beautiful.Life is an independent publication helping people design calmer, healthier, richer and more meaningful lives.

Our mission

Self-improvement content online is often extreme, contradictory or designed to sell you something. We believe a beautiful life is built from small, sensible, evidence-informed choices across eight areas: mind, body, money, love, home, purpose, adventure and growth. Our job is to make those choices easier with clear guides, free tools and introductions to trustworthy experts.

What we offer

  • Guides — practical articles grounded in published research and expert consensus.
  • Free tools — private, browser-based assessments, trackers and calculators.
  • Good news — a daily, automatically refreshed feed of uplifting stories.
  • Expert matching — free introductions to coaches, planners and wellness professionals.
  • Community — monthly contests that celebrate real beautiful moments.

Editorial standards

We write for clarity and accuracy, cite well-established research where relevant, avoid sensational claims and update content as evidence changes. Health and money articles are general information and always point readers to qualified professionals for personal advice. Sponsored content is always labelled and never influences our independent guides.

How we're funded

Beautiful.Life is free to read. We're funded by display advertising (Google AdSense), partner referrals from our matching service, sponsorships and voluntary reader support. Advertisers do not influence editorial content.

Corrections

Spotted an error? Please tell us via our contact page and we'll review it promptly.
