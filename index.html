FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Beautiful.Life free?

Yes. All articles and tools are free. The site is supported by advertising, partners and optional reader contributions.

Do your tools store my data?

No. The Wheel of Life, habit tracker, gratitude journal and other tools save information only in your own browser. We never see it.

How does "Get Matched" work?

You tell us your goal, budget and location. We review your request and introduce you to suitable professionals from our partner network. It's free and you're never obliged to hire anyone.

Is this medical or financial advice?

No. Our content is general information. Always consult a qualified professional about your specific circumstances.
